What is NSFAS?

NSFAS is a government scheme that provides financial aid to eligible South African students at public universities and TVET colleges. It can help with costs such as tuition and study-related expenses, depending on the current NSFAS rules.

Official information — eligibility, dates and amounts come only from nsfas.org.za.
General guidance — the steps and checklist here help you prepare, but aren't official.

How to apply

General guidance

1

Check the official requirements

Read the current eligibility criteria on the NSFAS website. Criteria and income limits can change each year.

2

Prepare your documents

Scan or photograph clear copies of everything on the checklist below before you start.

3

Create a myNSFAS account

Register on the official myNSFAS portal using your own email address and cellphone number.

4

Complete the application

Fill in your details carefully, upload your documents and submit before the official closing date.

5

Track your status

Log in to myNSFAS regularly to check your application status and respond to any requests.

6

Apply to institutions too

NSFAS funding is separate from admission. You must also apply to and be accepted by a university or TVET college.

Document checklist

General guidance — confirm the full list on the official NSFAS website.

  • Your South African ID document or birth certificate
  • ID documents of your parents, guardian or spouse (where applicable)
  • Proof of household income, if anyone in the household earns an income
  • Death certificate(s), if a parent is deceased (where applicable)
  • Disability Annexure A form, if you are applying with a disability
  • Any other documents requested by NSFAS during the application

Frequently asked questions

What is NSFAS?

The National Student Financial Aid Scheme is a government entity that provides financial aid to students from poor and working-class backgrounds to study at public universities and TVET colleges.

Does NSFAS guarantee me a place at a university?

No. NSFAS provides funding, but you must still apply to the university or college separately and meet their specific admission requirements.

When do applications open and close?

Dates change every year. Always check the official NSFAS website or your myNSFAS portal for the exact opening and closing dates for the current cycle.

Who qualifies for NSFAS?

Eligibility is based on household income (usually a combined income of R350,000 or less per year) and academic performance. Check the official criteria on the NSFAS site.

Can I apply before I get my matric results?

Yes, you can usually apply while awaiting your results. You will need to submit your results once they are released to finalize the application.

Is applying for NSFAS free?

Yes. Applying for NSFAS is completely free. Do not pay anyone who claims they can process your application for a fee.

Beware of scams

Applying for NSFAS is free. Only use the official NSFAS website and portal, and never share your password.
